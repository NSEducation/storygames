Mi’kma’ki · Nova Scotia

Four peoples. Four story games. One shared land.

In each game, you choose what happens. You will meet a Mi’kmaw family, an Acadian family, a Gaelic family and an African Nova Scotian family, and see how their stories touch. Play them in any order.

By Mark MacFadyen, Grade 7 English & Social Studies Teacher with the AVRCE, Nova Scotia · mmacfadyen@gnspes.ca

Every one of these stories takes place in Mi’kma’ki, the unceded land of the Mi’kmaq, who have lived here for about 13,000 years and live here today.

Stories that touch

These peoples have shared this land, and its hard history, for a very long time. Some people appear in more than one game. As you play, watch for Muin, Theresa and the Landry family, and notice what each person sees that the others do not.

Help us get these stories right

These stories were written with care and checked against sources, but they can still be made better. If you find a mistake, a word that should be different, or a part of a story that could be told more fairly, please let Mr. MacFadyen know. Email mmacfadyen@gnspes.ca. Students can also tell their teacher.

Each game follows one made-up family. No single story can stand for a whole people. Inside the games you will find real documents, History Notes, and ideas for where to learn more.
